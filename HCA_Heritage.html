The Anand Family Legacy

A century of leadership, innovation, and dedication to excellence

Hari Chand Anand

1867 – 1954

A visionary who turned collaboration into legacy

Hari Chand Anand was not only the visionary founder of HCA but also a man of warmth, wisdom, and resilience. Born in 1867, he built the foundation of our company on values of trust, collaboration, and mutual growth.

He believed that every person who works with HCA becomes part of a family. His philosophy was simple yet powerful: when we share responsibilities, no one carries the burden alone, and together, we all grow.

Visionary Leader: Built HCA to connect people and grow "as a family"
Collaborative Model: Innovative approach to shared workload and mutual growth
Historic Achievements: Celebrated as Asia's first non-white singer
Humanitarian Spirit: Remembered for generosity and helping those in need

Deewan Chand Anand

1910 – 2010

Discipline. Dedication. Legacy.

Deewan Chand Anand carried forward the vision of his father, Hari Chand Anand, with discipline and perseverance. Born in 1910 in Lahore, he received his early schooling there and later pursued engineering in Edler, Germany (1932–1939).

Known for his punctuality and uncompromising work ethic, Deewan Chand Anand enhanced his own abilities and dedicated his life to strengthening the company. Under his leadership, Harichand Anand & Co. continued to grow while staying rooted in the values of trust and responsibility.

Work Ethic: Known for exceptional punctuality and discipline
Growth: Expanded HCA with technical expertise and international perspective
Principles: Intolerance for mediocrity, commitment to excellence
Longevity: Stewarded the company through a century of life (1910-2010)

Anil Anand

Current Managing Director

Modernizing tradition with innovation

Mr. Anil Anand is the Managing Director of Hari Chand Anand & Co. (HCA), representing the third generation of the founding Anand family to lead the company. An Economics graduate, he joined HCA in the late 1970s and has steered the company's growth for over four decades.

Under his guidance, HCA has modernized and expanded its reach, entering international markets like Bangladesh and Ethiopia. He has traveled globally to learn about the latest sewing technologies, bringing back knowledge to improve HCA's offerings.

Global Expansion: Established operations in Bangladesh and Ethiopia
Partnerships: Built relationships with global sewing machine brands
Growth: Guided HCA through industry changes across decades
Mentorship: Training the fourth generation for business continuity

Bhavya Anand

Fourth Generation

Continuing the legacy into the future

Bhavya Anand represents the fourth generation of the Anand family, preparing to continue the legacy with innovation and dedication to excellence. He brings fresh perspectives and modern approaches while honoring the values that have defined HCA for over a century.

As the next generation leader, Bhavya is being groomed to take HCA into the future, ensuring that the company remains at the forefront of industrial sewing and automation technology while maintaining its family values and commitment to customer success.

Innovation: Bringing fresh perspectives to a century-old business
Technology: Focusing on digital transformation and modern solutions
Values: Honoring tradition while embracing innovation
Expansion: Exploring new markets and opportunities
Growth: Nurturing the company for future generations
