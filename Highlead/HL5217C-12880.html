HL5217C-12880 Lifting & 360 Degree Rotating Head Heavy Duty Templating Sewing Machine

Properties

  • The machine head has automatic lifting and rotating functions, lifting height 50mm
  • 360 ° infinite rotation, no reverse seaming, fast turning angle ,high efficiency
  • Large sewing range of 1280X800mm, X-Y Cartesian coordinate feeding, driven by the same servo motor
  • Independent servo motor drive for upper and lower axes, flexible control, high precision, and fast operation
  • The base design adopts integrated casting molding, which is steady, durable,high-precision, and has low vibration
  • 5217C has 3times LU-960 vertical hook reducing the frequency of bobbin replacement and improving production efficiency. The new oil supply system runs stably and is easy to adjust
  • Adopting a combination of stepper motor driven inner pressure foot and pneumatic outer pressure foot for easy adjustment
  • Cylinder driven straight knife thread trimmer
  • Fully functional: automatic cutting, automatic foot press foot, thread breakage detection, template recognition, E-tension, needle cooling system, independent quantitative bobbin winder, bottom thread alarm, loT device, etc
  • Convenient template automatic recognition and quick template clamping mechanism, stable pressure clamping , advanced computer control system, large-sized color touch
  • Display screen, easy and intuitive operation
  • Optional laser cutting, scanning, automatic bobbin changing, point pen, wire scanning and other functions
