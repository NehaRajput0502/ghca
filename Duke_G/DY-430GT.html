DY 430GT Direct Drive Lockstitch Electronic Bar Tacking Machine

Features & Highlights

  • Highest rotation speed in the industry: Maximum 3,200rpm
  • High-quality sewing consistent with sewing data
  • Low noise and vibration due to high rigidity frame and computer-balanced components
  • Sewing area: 40 x 30 mm, suitable for men's wear, jeans, knitted fabric, women’s underwear
  • Pattern editing software enables artistic sewing within 40 x 30 mm with high extensibility
  • Presser lifter height adjustable easily through operation panel, no tools required
  • Fastest cycling time with high speed sewing and beautiful stitch quality
  • Low power consumption with direct computer driving and small power-saving motor, reducing power by 35%
  • Thread sweeping, cutting, and clamping devices all standard
  • Flash memory storage with CF card support to increase sewing patterns
  • 50 user programs and 9 cycles with 15 programs each
  • Preloaded with 89 sewing patterns, expandable up to 200 sewing data patterns
