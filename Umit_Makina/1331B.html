Rest Engineered to Perfection

1331B - AUTOMATIC ERGONOMIC PLINTH COATING AND PUNCHING MACHINE

Properties

  • Touch screen easy operation
  • Double weld bonding feature
  • Ability to set each of the resources independently
  • Preparation table
  • Note: The weld part on the inside is used as gluing.
  • The weld part on the outside is overheated and is used to easily tear off the nylon when the process is finished.
  • When the resistances heat up, elongation occurs. In this case, the resistances are stretched and resistance breakage is prevented.
  • Two modes are used in the program.
  • Mode-1 is used for lower micron nylons.
  • Mode-2 is used for high micron nylons.
  • For safety, the sensor is protected in handshakes and does not heat up when the sticking button is not completely lowered, and when the foot is taken off the pedal, the button rises up again, preventing unwanted injuries.

Operating voltage:220 V 1 phase 2 Kw
