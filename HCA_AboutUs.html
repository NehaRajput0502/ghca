About Us


Since 1910, Harichand Anand & Co. has been synonymous with quality
craftsmanship and innovation in textiles and manufacturing.

Our Story

From a humble beginning in Lahore to becoming a trusted name in multiple industries, HCA has been shaping the sewing industry since 1910. Founded by Hari Chand Anand, the first Asian General Manager of Singer. The company laid strong foundations of expertise and innovation.
His legacy was carried forward by Mr. D.C. Anand, who trained at Adler, Germany (1933–1939) and successfully expanded the business from Lahore (undivided India) to Delhi after 1947. Today, under the guidance of Mr. Anil Anand, the third generation, HCA continues to grow as a pioneer in industrial sewing machines and automation. An Economics graduate, Mr. Anand has traveled the world exploring cutting-edge technologies, production techniques, and is also preparing the next generation to carry forward this vision.
With over 10 offices and a strong dealer network across India. HCA caters to diverse industries into garments, leather goods, upholstery, footwear, furniture, bedding, tents, parachutes, surgical equipment, car seat covers, ordnance, and many more. Backed by a team of trained professionals, HCA has built a reputation not just as a supplier, but as a solution provider for the sewing industry.
Our commitment remains clear: delivering the latest global technology, strong after-sales support, and “Making Automation Affordable” for all..

Overview

Hari Chand Anand & Co. (HCA) is not just a machinery supplier, we are solution partners for the garment and textile industry. Our expertise lies in creating end-to-end sewing and automation solutions that address real manufacturing challenges. From advanced sewing technologies for garments, leather, home furnishings, and technical textiles, to genuine spare parts, accessories, and intelligent automation systems, we design solutions that improve efficiency, reduce manual dependency, and drive sustainable growth. With a strong nationwide service network and a century of technical know-how, HCA works alongside every customer to deliver tailored, future-ready solutions that ensure long-term success.

Current mission

At HCA, our mission is to empower manufacturers by making automation solutions both accessible and impactful. We go beyond supplying equipment; we focus on enabling productivity, reducing reliance on manual labor, and supporting sustainable growth. Every solution we deliver is guided by one goal: helping our customers achieve lasting success in a fast-evolving industry.
