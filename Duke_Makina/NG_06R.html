Power in Precision.

Semi-Automatic Mattress Rolling Machine

Advantages

  • Highly adaptable — suitable for spring mattresses, sponge mattresses, or latex mattresses
  • Works in conjunction with a compressor: compression before wrapping for best results
  • No limit to mattress thickness; roll diameter determined by mattress size
  • Typical roll diameter: 28–35 cm (varies with thickness & length)
  • Produces firm, secure packaging that won’t expand after wrapping
  • Economical and aesthetically pleasing packaging outcome
  • Greatly reduces product volume, lowering transportation and storage costs
  • High production efficiency with simple operation — workers can learn in 5 minutes
  • CE certified, with stable performance and strong cost-effectiveness
  • Easy installation and convenient maintenance
