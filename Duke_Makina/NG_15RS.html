Power in Precision.

Full Automatic Mattress Rolling Machine (Adjustable Diameter)

Advantages

  • Adjustable roll diameter — size depends on mattress thickness and length
  • Wide application: suitable for spring, sponge, or latex mattresses
  • Supports both straight-roll and folding roll wrapping processes
  • High production efficiency (≈2 pcs/min)
  • Simple operation — workers can learn in just 3 minutes
  • Low packaging cost: can use 40-micron PE film (no heat seal) or 90-micron PE film (heat seal)
  • Perfect wrapping effect: firm, durable, aesthetic, and cost-effective
  • Easy to replace film rolls during production
  • After rolling, mattress volume is greatly reduced → lower transport & storage costs
  • Equipped with Siemens PLC control system & branded electrical components
  • Control panel supports multiple languages and remote system integration
  • Stable performance, easy to install, user-friendly maintenance

Efficiency:≈ 2 pcs/min
