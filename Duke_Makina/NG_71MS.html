Power in Precision.

Full Automatic Mattress Packing & Compress Machine

Advantages

  • Integrated system: mattress compression & packaging in one fully automatic line
  • Operates from inlet to outlet via conveyor belt for seamless production flow
  • Functions include automatic positioning, sensing, compression, three-sided hot sealing, and film trimming
  • Automatically cuts off excess film outside the sealing for a clean finish
  • Quick hydraulic system ensures stable, high-speed efficiency (100 pcs/h)
  • Simple operation — only requires two rolls of film (top & bottom) with auto photo detection and width sensing
  • Connects with front and back assembly lines for integrated automation
  • Reduces labor intensity, saves manpower cost, time, transport, and storage space
  • Certified with CE for high safety standards
  • Equipped with Siemens PLC control system and branded electrical components
  • Multi-language touch panel with remote system for easy monitoring, installation, and maintenance
