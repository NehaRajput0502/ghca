Power in Precision.

Automatic Sponge Gluing Integrated Assembly Line

Advantages

  • The gluing production line consists of an automatic gluing machine, an automatic lifting conveyor, an automatic cotton press, an automatic centering machine, an automatic edge trimming machine, an automatic rotating machine, and an automatic stacking machine.
  • The PLC, touch screen, frequency converter, remote module, etc., used in the machinery are all German Siemens brands.
  • The Siemens remote control system facilitates factory production capacity saving and allows access via mobile apps anytime.
  • Supports storage of after-sales data and enables direct remote control for timely handling of mechanical issues.
  • High degree of mechanical automation with automatic product height detection, trimming according to input data, and stacking multiple products.
  • The production process is more intuitive and monitorable, greatly improving production efficiency.
  • Short training cycle, simple operation, and easy staff shift management.
