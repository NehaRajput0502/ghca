Roll Packing machine

Advantages

  • Specially designed compressor for compressing and packaging sponge products such as sofa cushions and sofa bags.
  • Excellent compression function with stable performance.
  • Reduces transportation volume of sofa bags and saves storage space.
  • Lowers transportation costs by optimizing loading capacity.
  • Maintains the original shape and quality of sponge sofa bags after compression.
  • Greatly improves packaging efficiency and productivity.
  • High-value, reliable choice for sponge sofa manufacturers.
  • Workbench allows folding items to required width, with customization for widths beyond standard 53mm.
  • Feeding component with synchronized conveyor curtains ensures smooth material transport for rolling.
  • Rolling rods clamp material automatically and roll it precisely once detected by sensors.
  • Automatic push system moves finished wrapped products into packaging bags, ensuring consistent roll size controlled by upper and lower mold baffles.
