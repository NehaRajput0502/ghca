Automatic Spring Mattress Glue Spraying Assembly Machine (Single Line)

Advantages

  • The fully automatic glue spraying conveyor line is composed of 6 parts, including a conveyor belt workbench, an automatic centering machine, a hot melt glue spraying machine, a hot melt glue supply system, a flipping table, and a cotton press.
  • The PLC, touch screen, frequency converter, remote model, etc., used in the machinery are all German Siemens brands.
  • The machine is loaded with formula data, allowing customers to enter and save corresponding data in advance according to packaging and production requirements of each product.
  • Production can be started with one key by selecting saved data on the touch screen; the machine can be paused and resumed without restarting the entire packaging process.
  • The glue spraying gun has 4 gun heads divided into two groups for independent movement, providing multiple spraying trajectories.
  • The spray gun heads adapt to different product heights and automatically adjust.

Technical Parameters

Glue Spraying Efficiency:380V / 50Hz
Number of Spray Guns:3000 × 2300 mm

Rubber Hose Cotton Press Pressure:450–800 mm

Maximum Mattress Height:400 mm
Voltage:380V / 50Hz
Total Power:28 kW
Cotton Press Pressure:1.6T / 0.5MPa
