Full Automatic Mattress Compress Machine

Advantages

  • Integrated fully automatic mattress compressor with conveyor belt operation from inlet to outlet
  • Equipped with automatic positioning, photo-sensing, and three-sided heat sealing
  • Quick hydraulic system ensures high production efficiency
  • Simple operation — requires only two rolls of film (top & bottom) and automatic sensing of mattress width
  • Automatic photo detection and width sensing to optimize sealing and compression
  • Connects seamlessly with front and rear assembly lines for process automation
  • Saves labor, time, and production costs while reducing labor intensity
  • Greatly reduces transportation and storage volume, lowering logistics costs
  • Certified with CE for safety and compliance
  • Uses Siemens PLC control systems and electrical components
  • Control panel supports multiple languages and remote system integration
  • Easy installation, convenient maintenance, and stable operation
