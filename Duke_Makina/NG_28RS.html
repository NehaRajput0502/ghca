Power in Precision.

Full Automatic Mattress Compress Fold Roll & Packaging Machine

Advantages

  • Newly developed & upgraded full-automatic integrated line
  • Functions: wrapping, compressing, sealing, folding, and roll-packing
  • After compression, mattress can be folded in half and rolled to minimize volume
  • Multiple working modes available:
    • Standard plastic film wrapping
    • Pressing only
    • Rolling only
    • Wrap–Compress–Roll
    • Wrap–Compress–Fold–Roll
  • Can be combined with stacking machine for wrapping-only operations
  • Significant packaging cost saving:
    • Front compressor uses 0.07–0.09 mm plastic film
    • Final roll-packing uses 0.04 mm thin PE film
  • Flexible factory adaptation — line can be arranged straight or corner layout
  • Equipped with Siemens PLC, HMI & inverter system
  • Multi-language touch screen, remote diagnostics, and reliable after-sales support
  • CE certified with high safety and top-level automation
